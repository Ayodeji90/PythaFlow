## The problem, in one line

Every week, guests message your venue **after your team clocks out** — or during a
full dinner service when no one can reply — and every unanswered DM is a table that
goes to the place down the road.

## What the concierge does

- **Answers every guest instantly, 24/7** — on your WhatsApp, Instagram and website
  chat — in the guest's own language, in *your* venue's voice.
- **Answers only from *your* facts.** We load your menu, hours and policies; it never
  invents a price or makes a promise. If it doesn't know, it says a team member will
  follow up — and flags your staff.
- **Takes reservation requests and drafts them for your team.** **Nothing is confirmed
  without your staff's one-tap approval.** You stay in charge, always.
- **Hands off to a human** the moment a guest asks for one, or anything sensitive comes
  up.
- **Keeps working when the light goes.** It answers on the guest's phone from the
  cloud — so even if your venue's power or internet drops, your guests still get a reply.
